# indusind/default

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `indusind/default`
- **Official product:** **Not a single card.** Mail: `IndusInd Bank Credit Card`. Bank lists many live and discontinued products (Legend, Nexxt, EazyDiner, ePay Amex, Platinum Aura Edge, etc.).
- **defaultKind rationale:** `parser_fallback`. Named keys here: `auraedge`, `amex-epay`. Same mail subject as those variants — benefits **must not** be assumed.

## What we cannot claim

Aura Edge 8X plans, ePay per-transaction points, lounge, and fees are product-specific. IndusInd MITC_Premium.pdf is the bank schedule.

## Fees and Waivers (illustrative from MITC)

MITC lists **Platinum Aura Edge** and **ePay Amex** as **NIL joining / NIL annual**. Many other cards have fees. **Do not** apply NIL to an unknown IndusInd product.

- Priority Pass **not available** on Aura Edge, ePay Amex, Platinum, and a long exclusion list in MITC.
- Railway booking surcharge: Aura / Aura Edge “as applicable for other cards”.

## Rewards / Lounge / Insurance

**depends** on variant. See `indusind/auraedge` and `indusind/amex-epay`.
