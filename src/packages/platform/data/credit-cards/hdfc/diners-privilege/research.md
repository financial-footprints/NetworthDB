# hdfc/diners-privilege

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `hdfc/diners-privilege`
- **Official name:** **HDFC Bank Diners Club Privilege**. Mail: `Your HDFC Bank - Diners Club International Credit Card Statement`.

## Fees and Waivers

- Joining/renewal: **₹1,000 + tax**.
- Waiver: **₹3 lakh** annual spends.
- Forex: **3.5%**.
- **Fuel surcharge waiver: not applicable** (MITC).
- Interest-free up to 50 days.

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

- **4 RP per ₹200** retail.
- **20 RP per ₹200** on Swiggy/Zomato **online food** (5X vs 4). Additional 4X food RP **capped 2,500 RP/calendar month**.
- SmartBuy up to **10X**.
- Cycle cap **50,000 RP** (MITC).
- Redeem: SmartBuy travel **₹0.50**; exclusive catalogue up to **₹0.35**; restaurants **₹0.50**; cashback **₹0.20**.

## Accelerated Earn

- 5X Swiggy/Zomato food (with 2500 extra-RP cap). SmartBuy 10X.

## Lounge

- From **1 Jul 2026**: **3 lounge vouchers / calendar quarter** if **₹60,000** eligible spends in **previous calendar quarter** = **2 domestic + 1 international**.
- Older marketing still says 2 domestic / quarter — **use Jul 2026 rule**.

- BookMyShow weekend **BOGO**, max **₹250/ticket**, **2 free tickets / calendar month** (Fri–Sun shows).

- Welcome: **₹75,000 in first 90 days** → complimentary **Swiggy One + Times Prime** annual memberships.
- Quarterly: **₹1.5 lakh** → **₹1,500** voucher (Marriott / BBQ Nation / Lakme / O2 Spa / Decathlon / Dominos).
- Lounge spend ₹60,000 previous quarter (from Jul 2026).
- Annual fee waiver ₹3 lakh.

## Welcome / Renewal Benefits
