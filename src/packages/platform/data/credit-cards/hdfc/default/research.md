## What we cannot claim

Lounge quotas, 5X brands, NeuCoins, and Swiggy 10% cashback are **variant-specific**. Bank MITC: https://www.hdfcbank.com/personal/pay/cards/credit-cards/personal-mitc (English PDF).

## Fees (examples from business/personal MITC extracts — still variant-specific)

- Regalia / Regalia Activ family: **₹2,500**, waiver **₹3 lakh**/annum (Regalia) vs Gold **₹4 lakh**.
- Forex often **2% or 3.5%** by card.
- Fuel waiver often **₹400–₹5,000**, cap **₹500**/cycle; **Diners Privilege: fuel waiver NA**.
- RP validity typically **2 years**; unused **>365 days** nullifies points.
- Statement-cycle RP caps e.g. **50,000** for Regalia Gold / Regalia / Diners Privilege / Tata Neu Infinity.
- Education via CRED/Cheq etc. **no RP** from 1 Sep 2024 on most cards.
- Grocery / utility / telecom monthly RP caps (e.g. 2000) apply to many cards.
- Priority Pass visits **in India charged $27+tax** even on cards that have PP (Regalia/Gold/Tata Neu Infinity).
