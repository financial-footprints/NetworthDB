# hdfc/regalia-gold

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `hdfc/regalia-gold`
- **Official name:** **HDFC Bank Regalia Gold**. Mail: `Your HDFC Bank - HDFC Bank Regalia Gold Credit Card Statement`.
- Product page notes changes **effective 15 May 2026**.

## Fees and Waivers

- Joining/renewal: **₹2,500 + tax**.
- Waiver: spend **₹4 lakh** in 12 billing cycles of anniversary year.
- Forex: **2%** on FX spends (product page).
- Reward redemption fee **₹99 + tax** (fees page).
- Interest-free up to 50 days.

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

- **5 RP per ₹150** is **not** stated; page: **5 RP per ₹200** retail (also “4 RPs on daily and retail” in calculator — **prefer 5 RP / ₹200** from Reward Benefits section).
- **5X** on Myntra, Nykaa, Reliance Digital and more.
- SmartBuy: up to **10X** hotels/flights; **5X** instant vouchers.
- Cycle cap **50,000 RP**.
- Redemption: Gold catalogue **₹0.65**; flights/hotels **₹0.50**; airmiles up to 0.5; products/vouchers up to **₹0.35**; statement **₹0.20** (one table) / **₹0.15** (later table) — **conflict on cashback rate**.
- Travel redemption **70%** of booking; **50,000 RP/month** cap on statement cashback and on SmartBuy flights/hotels.

## Accelerated Earn

- 5X selected lifestyle brands; SmartBuy 10X.

## Lounge

- Domestic: **3 visits / calendar quarter** if spend **≥ ₹60,000** in **preceding calendar quarter** (track on SmartBuy). Page header also says “12 complimentary domestic” as value marketing.
- International: **6 complimentary / calendar year** via Priority Pass. PP in India **charged**.

## Travel / Hotel / Golf / Concierge

- **Boarding Edge:** spa / buffet / hotel upgrade / airport transfer — **any 2 per calendar quarter** via boarding pass on SmartBuy (DreamFolks).
- Dining: up to **10%** Swiggy Dineout Good Food Trail.
- Concierge / SmartBuy helpdesk numbers on page.

## Insurance

- Air accidental death **₹1 crore**.
- Emergency overseas hospitalisation **₹15 lakh**.
- Credit liability **₹9 lakh**.

## Milestones and Spend Thresholds

- Welcome: **₹1 lakh in first 90 days** → Swiggy One + MMT Black Gold.
- Quarterly: **₹1.5 lakh** → **₹1,500** Myntra/Nykaa vouchers.
- Annual: **₹5 lakh and ₹7.5 lakh** → **₹5,000** flight vouchers each milestone.
- Unlocked milestones: **60 days** to avail.

## Welcome / Renewal Benefits
