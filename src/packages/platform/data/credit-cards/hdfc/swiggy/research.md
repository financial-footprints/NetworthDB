# hdfc/swiggy

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `hdfc/swiggy`
- **Official name:** **Swiggy HDFC Bank Credit Card**. Mail: `Swiggy HDFC Bank Credit Card Statement`.
- **Transition:** bank is moving holders to **Swiggy Ornge** or **Swiggy BLCK**; current card benefits continue until migrated.
- Co-brand: Swiggy.

## Fees and Waivers

- Joining/renewal: **₹500 + tax** (product page). MITC PDF table OCR showed other numbers — **prefer product page ₹500**.
- Waiver: **₹2 lakh** anniversary spends (ex cash-on-call, BT, cash withdrawal).
- Cashback as **statement credit** for statements after **21 Jun 2024** (older Swiggy Money on app).

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

Cashback (not HDFC RP):

- **10%** on Swiggy app: Food, Instamart, Dineout, Genie — **up to ₹1,500 per billing cycle**. No CB on Swiggy Money wallet, Liquor, Minis.
- **5%** online (apparels, electronics, dept stores, personal care, local cabs, home décor, pet, pharmacy, discount stores, entertainment) — **up to ₹1,500 per billing cycle**. MCC list in T&Cs.
- **1%** other categories — **up to ₹500 per billing cycle**, except fuel, rent, EMI, wallet, jewellery, government.
- No cashback on txns **< ₹100**.
- Golf marketing on page (4 complimentary rounds / 12 lessons) — **unusual for this SKU; confirm T&C** (may be leftover template). Mark **unverified / possible page contamination**.

## Accelerated Earn

## Welcome / Renewal Benefits

## Exclusions and Caps
