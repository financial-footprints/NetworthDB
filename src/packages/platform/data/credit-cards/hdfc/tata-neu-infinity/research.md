# hdfc/tata-neu-infinity

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `hdfc/tata-neu-infinity`
- **Official name:** **Tata Neu Infinity HDFC Bank Credit Card**. Mail: `Your HDFC Bank - Tata Neu Infinity HDFC Bank Credit Card Statement`.
- Networks: Visa / RuPay (lounge rules differ). Co-brand Tata Neu / NeuPass.

## Fees and Waivers

- Joining/renewal: **₹1,499 + tax**. Joining levied on **120th day**; renewal on anniversary.
- Waiver: **₹3 lakh** before renewal date.
- Limited LTF window **1 Oct–31 Dec 2024** digital/physical — historical.
- Fuel: **1%** waiver on **₹400–₹5,000**, max **₹500 per statement cycle**.
- Interest-free up to 50 days.

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

**NeuCoins** (not HDFC RP). Transferred to Tata Neu within **7 working days** of statement.

Non-UPI:

- **5%** NeuCoins on non-EMI Tata Neu + partner Tata brands.
- **1.5%** on other non-Tata and merchant EMI.
- **Additional 5%** on selected Tata Neu categories after NeuPass; from **15 Jan 2025** only on **primary cardholder’s mobile** Tata Neu account. Bill Pay, Tanishq, Cult.fit, Air India, Tata Play **excluded** from extra 5%.

UPI (from 1 Aug 2024):

- **0.50%** on eligible UPI (posted by bank) + **1% extra** via Tata Neu UPI ID (posted to Tata Neu) = up to **1.5%**.
- **All UPI NeuCoins capped at 500 / calendar month**.

Caps / exclusions:

- No NeuCoins: fuel, wallet, rental, government, online gaming; skill gaming MCC 5816 from **1 Jul 2025**.
- Grocery **2,000**/month; utility **2,000**/month; telecom/cable **2,000**/month; insurance **2,000**/month (from 1 Jul 2025, was 2000/day).
- Education via CRED/Cheq etc. **no** NeuCoins; direct school/college OK.
- Cycle cap **50,000** NeuCoins (MITC).
- Validity from **1 Aug 2025**: **12 months** from end of credit month. Pre-Aug-25 non-promo balance set to **31 Jul 2026**.

## Accelerated Earn

- Extra 5% NeuPass categories; UPI Tata Neu ID +1%.

## Redemption

- Tata Pay / NeuCoins on Tata Neu (Bigbasket, Croma, Westside, CLiQ, IHCL, 1mg, Titan/Tanishq via Neu, etc.). Closest-expiry coins first.

## Lounge

Domestic (Visa/RuPay) from **10 Jun 2025**: **2 lounge vouchers / quarter** (max **8/year**) on **₹50,000** calendar-quarter spends. Claim portal **120 days**; voucher use **180 days**. Upgraded/reissued card: **only new card spends**.

International Visa: Priority Pass **on request**; **4 visits/year (1/quarter)** outside India; extra **USD 27 + GST**; guests always charged; **PP in India charged**.

International RuPay: **4/year (1/quarter)**; **$3.25** fee reversed on complimentary visits.

## Travel / Hotel / Golf / Concierge

- Lounge as above. Golf **unknown**.

## Insurance

- Air accidental death **₹1 crore**.
- Emergency overseas hospitalisation **₹15 lakh**.
- Lost card liability **₹9 lakh**.

## Milestones and Spend Thresholds

- Lounge ₹50k/quarter. Fee waiver ₹3 lakh.

## Welcome / Renewal Benefits

- **1,499 NeuCoins** (fee reversal) if **first txn within 30 days**; claim in app within **60 days** of txn (eligible window **180 days** from statement). Validity **365 days** from claim. **Not** for LTF/FYF cards.

## Exclusions and Caps

- See NeuCoins exclusions and monthly caps. UPI 500/month.
