# pnb/default

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `pnb/default`
- **Official product:** **Not a single card.** Mail: `Your PNB Credit Card Statement for the month`. PNB issues Visa Classic/Gold/Platinum/Signature, RuPay Platinum/Select/Millennial, Luxura, Prakram, etc.
- **defaultKind rationale:** `parser_fallback`. `pnb/platinum` shares the **same handler** but is researched as Visa/RuPay Platinum.

## What we cannot claim

Lounge on Luxura, Signature fees, and Platinum annual ₹500 must not be applied blindly. MITC: https://pnbcard.in/PDF/MITC.PDF and https://creditcard.pnb.bank.in/

## Fees (bank MITC snapshot)

- Visa Classic: joining/annual **NIL**.
- Visa Gold: annual **₹300** with usage conditions.
- **Visa Platinum:** joining **NIL**, add-on **₹500**, annual **₹500**.
- Visa Signature: joining **₹1500**, annual **₹2000**, waiver if spends **₹3 lakh**.
- RuPay Platinum: joining **NIL**, annual **₹500**, waiver if used **once each quarter**.
- PQCC programme: **NIL joining** for pre-qualified digital issuance (pnbindia.in).
