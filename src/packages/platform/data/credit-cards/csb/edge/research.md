## Fees and Waivers

MITC updated **5 Nov 2025**, charges from **6 Dec 2025** (ex-GST; GST extra):

- Joining: Edge **NIL**; Edge+ **₹999**.
- Annual membership (primary): **Nil** (table: “Nil … On completion of one year…” — wording is awkward; treat as **no annual fee listed as a rupee amount**; confirm live MITC).

- Forex: **3.5%**; DCC: **1.0%**.
- Overlimit: **not allowed / Nil**.
- Late payment: **5% of outstanding**, min **₹250**, max **₹2000**.
- Interest: **3.75% monthly / 45% annual** if full bill unpaid.
- Fuel surcharge: ₹10 up to ₹400 txn, else 1% above ₹400; **waiver 1% max ₹100** if spend **₹400–₹3,000** (ex GST).
- Fuel transaction fee: **1%** above ₹10,000 **or** monthly spends > ₹50,000.
- Rent: **1% min ₹149**.
- Wallet top-up: **1%**.
- Education (third-party): **1%**.
- Utility: **1%** if txn > ₹20,000 or monthly spends > ₹50,000.
