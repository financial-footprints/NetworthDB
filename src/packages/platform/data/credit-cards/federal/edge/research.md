# federal/edge

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `federal/edge`
- **Official name:** **Edge-Federal Credit Card** (Jupiter / Amica Neo Marketing). Mail: `Edge Federal Bank Credit Card Statement`.
- **Network:** not stated in MITC heading (Jupiter Edge cards are typically RuPay). **unknown** if Visa also exists.

## Fees and Waivers

Federal-Edge MITC (charges ex-GST):

- Joining: **Nil**.
- Annual (primary): **₹999**, **waived if ₹1.2 lakh** eligible spends in preceding year.
- Add-on annual: **Nil**. Issuance: **Nil**. Replacement: **₹249**. Closure: **Nil**.
- **No cash withdrawals**.
- Forex: **2%**.
- Overlimit: not allowed (Nil if merchant presents higher amount).
- Late payment: **2% of outstanding**, min **₹100**.
- Interest: **3.75% monthly (45% p.a.)** if full bill unpaid.
- Fuel surcharge: 1% of txn or ₹10, whichever higher; **waiver on fuel spends up to ₹4,000 per billing cycle**.

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

- **Jewels** on Jupiter app.
- **2 Jewels per ₹100** on qualified spends (min txn **₹100**).
- Max **15,000 Jewels per billing cycle**.
- Cash value: **5 Jewels = ₹1**.
- Booster pack: **10 Jewels / ₹100** on chosen MCC category (still 15,000 cap).
- Redeem: digital gold on Jupiter **or cash to Federal Bank account**.
- MAD unpaid → jewels **locked** and earning **paused**.
- Do not expire except unused **>365 days**, plus clawback cases.

## Accelerated Earn

- Category booster pack (10 Jewels/₹100) if purchased — optional, not base.

## Redemption

- Digital gold / bank cash. Conversion **5 Jewels = ₹1**.
