# icici/amazon

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `icici/amazon`
- **Official name:** **Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card**. Co-brand Amazon Pay (India). **Invite-only**.
- **Mail:** `Amazon Pay ICICI Bank Credit Card Statement for the period` and generic ICICI subject.
- Rewards settle as **Amazon Pay balance** (statement “Earnings transferred to Amazon Pay balance”).

## Fees and Waivers

Product page + Amazon MITC (updated **10 Jul 2026**):

- Joining **Nil**; annual **Nil**; add-on fees **Nil** (MITC schedule).
- Forex **1.99%**.
- Interest **3.75% p.m. / 45% p.a.** (w.e.f. 15 Nov 2024).
- Fuel surcharge: ICICI passes merchant surcharge; **1% waiver on all petrol pumps** (Amazon Pay card). GST on surcharge not reversed on off-us.
- Cash at branch: **₹100** → **₹150** w.e.f. **15 Jan 2026**.
- Government txns in utility bucket **does not apply** to this card (MITC note from Feb 2024).

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

Credited to **Amazon Pay balance**, **unlimited, never expire** (marketing):

- **5%** Amazon.in for **Prime** members; **3%** non-Prime (stated as points per ₹100: 5 / 3).
- **2%** on 100+ Amazon Pay partner merchants via **Login and Pay with Amazon**.
- **1%** other spends outside Amazon.
- Earnings appear after billing cycle, gift card to Amazon within **~2 working days**.

## Accelerated Earn

- Prime vs non-Prime Amazon rates. Partner 2%.

## Redemption

- Amazon / Amazon Pay checkout. Not a generic ICICI catalogue.

## Lounge

**unknown / not advertised** (LTF cards typically no lounge).

## Travel / Hotel / Golf / Concierge

- **3 months no-cost EMI** on eligible Amazon.in txns (FAQ). Golf/lounge **NA/unknown**.

## Insurance

**unknown** on product FAQ extract.

## Milestones and Spend Thresholds

- None for fees (LTF). Reward **uncapped** per marketing.

## Welcome / Renewal Benefits

- LTF; MITC: no joining/annual benefits when fees are nil.

## Exclusions and Caps

- Earnings only to **card owner’s** Amazon account. Full exclusion list in Amazon Pay T&C PDF.
