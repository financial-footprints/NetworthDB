# icici/coral

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `icici/coral`
- **Official name:** **ICICI Bank Coral Credit Card**. Mail same as default. RuPay variant has extra insurance/concierge.
- Networks: Visa/Mastercard/RuPay/Amex variants exist; Amex BookMyShow cashback **revoked 1 Jan 2025**.

## Fees and Waivers

- Joining **₹500 + GST**; annual **₹500 + GST**.
- Annual waived if spend **> ₹1.5 lakh** next year.
- Forex **3.5%**.
- ATM / revolving: **3.75% p.m.**
- LPC **₹100–₹1,300**.

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

- **2 RP per ₹100** retail.
- **1 RP per ₹100** utility and insurance.
- Redeem: health, travel, vouchers, appliances, electronics, fashion (catalogue).

## Accelerated Earn

- Milestone points (below). Movie/entertainment offers are discounts, not RP.

## Redemption

- Catalogue as above. Conversion rate **unknown** on product page.

## Lounge

- **1 domestic airport lounge / quarter** if spend **≥ ₹75,000** in **preceding calendar quarter** (following quarter access). **Primary only**.
- **4 railway lounge visits / year** (page also: **1 railway lounge / quarter**). Over-quota charged by lounge.

## Travel / Hotel / Golf / Concierge

- BookMyShow/INOX: **25% off up to ₹100**, twice a month; from **1 Apr 2026** movie/events complimentary-ticket offer needs **₹25,000** in preceding spends quarter (primary and plus cards each).
- RuPay: **24×7 concierge** 18002670731.

## Insurance

- RuPay: **₹2 lakh** personal accident.

## Milestones and Spend Thresholds

- **2,000 RP at ₹2 lakh** spend + **1,000 RP per additional ₹1 lakh**, max **10,000 RP per anniversary year**.
- Lounge ₹75k/quarter. Movies ₹25k/quarter (from Apr 2026). Fee waiver ₹1.5 lakh.

## Welcome / Renewal Benefits

- ₹500 joining. Renewal ₹500 unless ₹1.5 lakh spend.

## Exclusions and Caps

- Fuel waiver **only HPCL** on **ICICI merchant swipe**, **1%** on **₹400–₹4,000**.
- Milestone max 10k bonus RP/year.
