# icici/platinum

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `icici/platinum`
- **Official name:** **ICICI Bank Platinum Chip Credit Card** (product pages use “Platinum Chip”). Mail same as default.
- Entry-level lifetime-free ICICI card — **not** HPCL Platinum / MakeMyTrip Platinum / Adani Platinum.

## Fees and Waivers

- Joining **Nil**; annual **Nil** (product page). MITC: Nil/Nil; footnote some sourced at **₹99**.
- Forex **3.5%**.
- Revolving/ATM **3.75% p.m.** LPC ₹100–₹1300.

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

- **2 RP / ₹100** retail.
- **1 RP / ₹100** utility & insurance.
- Fuel: **no RP** typical (bank blog: rewards except fuel).

## Accelerated Earn

**unknown** (no milestone table on Platinum Chip page).

## Redemption

- Same catalogue families as Coral. Rate **unknown**.

## Lounge

- Product page **does not** list airport or railway lounge. MITC lounge spend rule lists Platinum Chip in a bundled sentence with Coral — **conflict**. Prefer **product page: no lounge marketed**; treat complimentary lounge as **unknown / likely NA**.

## Travel / Hotel / Golf / Concierge

**unknown** / not advertised.

## Insurance

**unknown** on Platinum Chip page (Coral RuPay has PA).

## Milestones and Spend Thresholds

**unknown**.

## Welcome / Renewal Benefits

- Lifetime free. “Cards with no joining/annual fee have no joining/annual benefits” (MITC).

## Exclusions and Caps

- Fuel surcharge waiver **1%** on **₹400–₹4,000 at HPCL** (product FAQ).
