# idfc/default

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `idfc/default`
- **Official product:** **Not a single card.** Mail: `Credit Card Statement`. IDFC FIRST has Millennia, Classic, Select, Wealth, WOW!, WOW! Black, co-brands, etc.
- **defaultKind rationale:** `parser_fallback`. Named travel/FD card in this repo is `idfc/wow`.

## What we cannot claim

WOW!’s 0% forex, 4X earn, and FD backing do **not** apply to Classic/Select/Wealth. Bank MITC is a **multi-variant schedule**.

## Fees and Waivers (bank MITC)

- Many unsecured cards: joining/annual **Nil** (Millennia/Classic/Select/Wealth/Employee/WOW!).
- Others: Power ₹199, SWYP ₹499, Hello Cashback ₹1000, Ashva ₹2999, Mayura ₹5999, WOW! Black ₹750, Gaj ₹12,500, Private ₹25,000, etc.
- Forex markup: **0%** on WOW! / WOW! Black / Private / Diamond Reserve / Mayura / Gaj; **1.99%** Select; **1.50%** Wealth; **3.50%** default for “all cards except…”.
- Reward redemption fee **₹99**.
- Fuel surcharge waiver **1%** on ₹200–₹5000; **caps vary** (WOW! group **₹100**/cycle in the waiver table).
- WOW! / WOW! Black / EARN / Hello Cashback / Quantum+: **no over-limit facility**.
- Reward points on Classic/Millennia/Select/Wealth/Family/**WOW!**: valid **24 months** from credit (existing balance as of June 2026 cycle valid to **June 2028**). Product FAQ for WOW still says “evergreen” — **MITC 24-month rule prevails**.
