# bob/default

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `bob/default`
- **Official product:** **Not a single card.** Mail subjects `E-statement for your BOB` / `BOBCARD` / duplicate BOB Card. BOBCARD Limited (ex BOB Financial) issues Easy, Select, Premier, Eterna, co-brands, etc.
- **defaultKind rationale:** `parser_fallback`. Named everyday product in this repo is `bob/easy`.

## What we cannot claim

Lounge, cashback %, joining fee, and rewards **depend on the BOBCARD variant**. Point users at https://www.bobcard.co.in/most-important-terms-and-condition and the specific product page.

## Fees and Waivers

Bank-level MITC PDF (`BOBCARD_MITS.pdf`) lists **per-product** joining and annual fees, e.g. Easy ₹500, Select ₹700, Premier ₹1000, Eterna ₹2499, many NIL products. **Do not** apply Easy’s ₹500 to unknown cards.

Add-on: MITC states **no joining/annual fee for add-on** (generic sentence in fee table).
