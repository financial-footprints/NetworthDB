# yes/ace

## Identity

- **Registry key:** `yes/ace`
- **Official name:** YES BANK **ACE** Credit Card (formerly **YES Prosperity Rewards Plus**). Mail subject: `Your YES_BANK_ACE Rupay Credit Card E-Statement` — RuPay network on this statement path.
- **Co-brand:** none (YES BANK retail).

## Fees and Waivers

YES BANK Key Fact Statement V_14_30-06-25:

- Joining / first year: **₹499 + GST**; renewal **₹499 + GST**.
- Waiver: spend **₹5,000 within first 30 days** → first-year membership waived; spend **₹50,000 in anniversary year** → next year’s renewal waived.
- Revolving interest: **3.99% p.m. (47.88% annualized)** (non-FD ACE). ACE against FD: **2.49% p.m.**
- Forex conversion: **2.75%** (ACE / ACE FD group).
- Fuel surcharge waiver cap: **₹100** per statement cycle (ACE).
- Other bank-wide charges (LPC, rent, utility, fuel >₹10k) as in `yes/default` research.

## Reward Currency and Base Earn

ACE Digital Welcome Kit (Nov 2023, yesbank.in):

- **8 YES Rewardz / ₹200** online shopping (except select categories).
- **4 YES Rewardz / ₹200** offline (except select).
- **2 YES Rewardz / ₹200** on select categories (mobile recharge, utility, insurance, education, etc.).
- **Cap: 5,000 YES Rewardz per statement cycle.**
- No Rewardz on: rent, wallet, fuel, government, marketing/advertising.
- Annual bonus rewards mentioned in kit — exact grid **unknown** in this extract (kit header only).

## Accelerated Earn
